Get the App

Wannabe Cup (WBC) golf tournament app · Last updated August 31, 2026

The app and this website are the same thing — the same leaderboard, the same scorecards, the same seventeen years of history. Installing it puts WBC on your home screen and keeps you signed in.

You will need the event password. It is the same one the website asks for, and a director has it. You enter it once, claim your name off the roster once, and never see either screen again.

Only having a look? Tap “Live Leaderboard — No account needed” on the sign-in screen. That opens the whole app read-only, with no password and no account at all.

Android

The Android app is distributed by invitation rather than through a public listing — it is not in the Play Store to be searched for, and there is nothing about a private golf tournament a stranger could use.

Tap here if you got the invite

It has to be the right Google account. The invite works only for the address we added, and that address must be the one signed into the Play Store on the phone you are holding — check Settings → Google if you are not sure. A work account on the list and a personal account on the phone produces “item not found”, which reads like the app is broken and is not. Tell a director which address you actually use and it takes ten seconds to fix.

Once it is installed it updates itself, like any other app on your phone.

iPhone

Not on the App Store yet. Until it is, the website installs to your home screen and behaves the same way:

  • Open wannabecup.com in Safari — not Chrome, which has no way to do this.
  • Tap the Share button, the square with the arrow out of the top.
  • Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Open it from the home screen icon from then on, not from a Safari tab.

Anything else

Just use the website. It is the whole app in a browser, on a laptop or any phone, with nothing to install.
